My college graduation topic
It's about skin disease identification.

There are several types of skin diseases:
1. Verruca 2. Seborrheic dermatitis 3. Psoriasis 4. Hives 5. Paederus dermatitis 6. Acne 7. Onychomycosis 8. Chickenpox 9. Alopecia

We divided the system functions into four main modules: Scan, Map, Graph, and News.
1. Skin Recognition (Scan): After processing by our system, the uploaded image will be analyzed, and the four most similar skin diseases will be listed, along with their visual symptoms and suggested treatments.
2. Map: Provides the locations of nearby medical institutions and pharmacies related to dermatological conditions, enabling users to conveniently seek medical consultation or purchase related medications.
3. Graph Analysis (Graph): Retrieves open government data, extracts statistical information about relevant diseases, and presents the results through visualized charts, allowing users to better understand their distribution.
4. Related News (News): Displays recent news related to skin health, offering users additional medical knowledge.
